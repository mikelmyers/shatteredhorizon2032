## Summary

The **structural level is complete and reference-grounded**: authentic Taoyuan geography
(narrow beach backed by mountains, Haihu/Linkou-style), a full Taiwanese town (27 building
blocks + roads + market + temple + school + props — verified, not empty), a fortified contested
beach (sea wall + 82 tetrapods + 40 dragon's-teeth + existing hedgehogs/wire/bunkers/towers/
landing-craft), and an urban combat layer (39 checkpoints + 142 rubble). The systems, feel,
gunplay, AI fairness, HUD feedback, and the full free-asset pipeline are done and validated live.

The remaining work is **not deferred by choice** — each item is gated on something an autonomous
agent provably cannot do: the human eye (look/feel), or assets behind a login / generated by
local AI tools. Where I could prove a gate, I did (see Wave 7).
